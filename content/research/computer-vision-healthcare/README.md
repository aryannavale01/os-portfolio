# Computer Vision for Healthcare — Research Notes

Notes on medical imaging workflows and vision/ML applied to healthcare.

## DICOM Sharing Platform

- Built a multi-hospital DICOM image sharing platform.
- Focus: secure transfer, standards compliance, and structured storage.
- Lesson: interoperability is the hard 80% of medical software.

## Vision & Edge ML

- Object detection and segmentation for clinical/diagnostic contexts.
- Model size versus latency trade-offs on edge hardware.

## Datasets & Evaluation

- Small, noisy medical datasets demand strong augmentation and careful split
  design.
- Sensitivity/specificity matter more than raw accuracy in diagnostics.
